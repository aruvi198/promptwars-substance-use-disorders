Immediate Grounding Box

Try this calming pattern for 1 minute:

  1. Inhale slowly through your nose for 4 seconds.
  2. Hold your breath gently for 4 seconds.
  3. Exhale slowly for 4 seconds.
  4. Hold again for 4 seconds.

If this feels overwhelming, focus on one slow breath and reach out to someone you trust.
